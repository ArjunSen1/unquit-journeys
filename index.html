UNQUIT® JOURNEYS
Your evidence

Welcome to UNQUIT® Journeys.

Here are moments from the lives of people. Some you may know. Many you will not. Different lives, different circumstances, but each arrived at a moment when quitting became an option.

You are invited to step into that moment before you know what happened next. Live it for a few minutes. Notice what comes up. Decide how you would navigate it.

Then begin.

Their moment. Your decision.

You do not become them. You stand where they stood.

Each journey brings you to a moment when someone had to decide what came next. The situation is theirs. Every decision is yours. You are not trying to guess what they did, and there is no right answer. You answer from your own life.

Across seven steps, you notice what you feel, find the evidence you already carry and decide what you would do next. A helper is there whenever you want one. Then time moves forward and their story returns beside yours, with your words in red.

The point is not whether you chose what they chose. It is what your choices reveal about you.

Each journey takes about twelve minutes. Nothing you write leaves this device. At the end, you lock in one sentence beginning with I did. That sentence stays with you under Your evidence.
